Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to use SportsSwap?

No. You can browse the marketplace and the community feed without one. An account is only needed to post listings, message people, save items, follow, or join groups.

How do I reset my password?

On the sign-in screen, enter your email and tap "Forgot password?". We'll email you a reset link. You can also change your password from Settings → Change password.

How do I change my username?

Settings → Username. Usernames can be changed once every 30 days.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap the flag icon on any post or listing to report it. To block someone, open their profile and tap Block — their posts and messages disappear from your app straight away. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile and data. If you have trouble, email us and we'll delete it for you.

Is it safe to buy and sell?

SportsSwap is a venue — sales are arranged directly between buyers and sellers. Meet in public places, check items before paying, and rate people after a deal so others know who to trust.

How do I create or join a group?

From the Community tab, tap "Create group" or "Join with code". Private groups need a join code from whoever created them.

Report a problem

Found a bug, or content that breaks our rules? Email us with:

  • What happened and what you expected
  • Your username
  • Your device and iOS version
  • A screenshot if you have one

For objectionable content or abusive behaviour, use the in-app flag or block first — that reaches us immediately.
